# Evora brand assets

Generated by `scripts/brand/build.mjs` (SVG is the source; PNGs are exports).
Visual identity is unchanged: a dark, monochrome, modern studio look. Only the name/logo changed.

## Colors

| Token | Hex       | Use                                             |
| ----- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Ink   | `#0B0B0F` | Main dark background; logo on light backgrounds |
| Paper | `#F4F4F5` | Logo on dark backgrounds; light background      |

The logo is always single-color: Paper on dark, Ink on light. Do not add gradients, outlines, or other colors to it.

## Construction

- Lowercase, geometric, single-weight letters drawn on a 100-unit x-height with a 16-unit stroke.
- The wordmark is 506 × 100 units. The symbol is the wordmark's "e".

## Clear space

- Keep empty space on every side equal to **half the x-height** (50 units at 100 units tall). For example, if the wordmark is 40 px tall, leave 20 px.
- For the symbol, keep empty space equal to **25% of its width**.

## Minimum size

- Wordmark: **16 px tall** on screen (about 80 px wide). Use the symbol for anything smaller.
- Symbol / app icon: **16 px** on screen. The 32 px favicon is the smallest raster export.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch, rotate, re-letter, or retype the wordmark in a font. Use the SVG.
- Don't put the logo on busy images without an Ink scrim.
- Don't use the previous brand's logo alongside the Evora logo.
